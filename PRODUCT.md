# Product

## Register

product

## Users
The builder and a small dev team. They are developing the marketplace's search engine, not the marketplace website. They use the console to throw rough requests at the engine and see exactly why it ranked what it ranked.

## Product Purpose
A test console for the freelance-search engine: build a request (text, tags, reference image), inspect or hand-edit the SearchSpec, read the ranked results with their feature contributions and explanations, and see who trust & safety held back and why. Success means a ranking decision never needs the source code to be explained.

## Brand Personality
None beyond "instrument". The website's identity is out of scope for now. The console stays quiet, legible, and exact.

## Anti-references
None specified. Default to avoiding decoration that competes with the numbers.

## Design Principles
- The numbers are the interface: every score shown is a real logged feature, never a decorative stand-in.
- One screen for one loop: request, spec, results, and trust in view together, without navigation.
- Honest states: degraded mode, held accounts, below-floor candidates, and spec errors are shown, never hidden.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-operable controls, reduced-motion respected. Thai and English input.
